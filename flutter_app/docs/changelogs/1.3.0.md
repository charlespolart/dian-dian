# What's new — 1.3.0 (2026-10-14)

Store release notes, pasted as-is in App Store Connect and the Play Console
(one block per store locale, same bullets in every language, 500 characters
max per block — the Play Console limit).

## en-US
- Much faster: trackers and the tracker list now load instantly, and switching years no longer waits on the network.
- The app opens right away where you left off, even offline, and refreshes silently once you're back online.
- Changes made on another device now show up without reloading, including legend order and colors.
- Fixed: days in December could not be saved.
- Fixed: occasional unexpected logouts.

- L'app s'ouvre là où vous l'aviez laissée, même hors ligne, puis se met à jour discrètement une fois connectée.
