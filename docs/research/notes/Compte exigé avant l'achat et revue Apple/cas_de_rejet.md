# App Store Review rejections for requiring registration or sign-in before an in-app purchase: the cases, how they ended, and what App Review treats as "account-based" (as of November 25, 2026)

Scope: first-hand reports of App Review rejections for requiring registration or sign-in before an in-app purchase (IAP), a subscription, a restore, or access to purchased features; how each ended; the wording of the rejection over the years; and what App Review counts as "account-based". Sources: Apple Developer Forums (threads re-opened and read in full, including 724336, 731471, 819232 and the thread it redirects to, 818946), RevenueCat community, Microsoft Q&A, GitHub pull requests, and Chinese (Jianshu, V2EX), Japanese (Qiita, teratail, personal blogs, a no-code forum) and Korean/French searches. All pages were read on 2026-11-25 unless marked [SEARCH]. Forum pages show only month and year, so dates are given at that precision. Labels: [PRIMARY] = Apple's own text (guidelines, HIG, developer docs, Apple staff) or a first-hand account of a rejection with the message quoted; [DOCS] = other official platform or SDK documentation; [VENDOR] = a vendor with a commercial interest; [COMMENTARY] = blogs, forums or practitioners without primary evidence, including first-hand accounts where Apple's message is only paraphrased; [SEARCH] = known from a search snippet only; [INFERENCE] = my own reasoning. This note builds on `notes/Utiliser l'app sans compte/monetisation_regles_stores.md` §7–8 and does not repeat its guideline analysis.

## 1. The rejection texts and how each case ended

### Takeaway
I found 27 purchase-related cases from May 2015 to October 2026. Apple's written text is quoted in 19 of them, and the reviewer's words are reported from phone calls in 3 more. The message is a fixed template. It hinges on "register with personal information", "not account based" and "must be optional or tied to account-specific functionality". It tells the developer to let people buy first and to present registration as an optional way to reach the purchase on other devices. Of the 27 cases, 6 were approved or released after making login optional or adding a guest purchase (for one of them, case 8, the author says only "ready to publish"). Four were approved after a written explanation, with no change to the account requirement reported (2015, 2020, 2022, 2025). Three changed the app but did not report approval, and the remaining 14 have no reported outcome. No case reports an App Review Board appeal won or lost on this purchase template.

### Cited Findings
- **The current template (2023–2026).** "We noticed that your app requires users to register with personal information to purchase in-app purchase products that are not account based. Apps cannot require user registration prior to allowing access to app content and features that are not associated specifically to the user. User registration that requires the sharing of personal information must be optional or tied to account-specific functionality." Next Steps: "please revise your app to not require users to register before purchasing in-app purchase products that are not account based. You may explain to the user that registering will enable them to access the purchased content from any of their iOS devices and provide them a way to register at any time, if they wish to later extend access to additional devices." Then: "Please note that although App Store Review Guideline 3.1.2 requires an app to make subscription content available to all the iOS devices owned by a single user, it is not appropriate to force user registration to meet this requirement; such user registration must be optional." In the copies that include it (Dec 2022, Feb 2023, Mar 2025), the Resources line reads: "See guideline 5.1.1(v) - Account Sign-In to learn more about our requirements for apps with account-based content and features." — [PRIMARY] — [forum 729708](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/729708), [forum 731471](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/731471), [millionblog](https://millionblog.org/appstore-review-5-1-1-data-collection-and-storage/), [forum 724336](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/724336). Transfer: the planned screen ("Create your account to continue. Premium is tied to your account") is the exact behaviour the template names.
- **App Review Board answer (Feb 2023, slots game with server-held coins), quoted by the developer:** "Although requesting that users register to for tracking users' purchases and game progresses is acceptable, requiring users to fully register for an account before entering the game is not. Users should be allowed to access none account-based features before registration and login. You may also choose to alert users that accessing game without binding an account may result in losing game progress or in-app purchase items." Also: "Once the user decides to use account-based features, the app may present the registration or login feature at that time." The developer then asked whether a guest must be able to buy. The forum gave no answer, and the outcome is unknown. — [PRIMARY] — [forum 724336](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/724336). Transfer: a warning that Premium or data may be lost without an account is explicitly allowed. Requiring an account is not.
- **Memory journal, May 2023 (closest to the planned app).** "I have an app that prompts a user to Continue With Apple at launch before proceeding… They can only record 1 memory a day. Once 3 memories are recorder… they are prompted with an upgrade option through an IAP option". The app got the full template. The developer asked "Is this still not allowed or is this something I have to explain to them and fight for?" and got no answer. — [PRIMARY] — [forum 729708](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/729708). Transfer: Sign in with Apple, which can hide the e-mail, was still treated as "register with personal information". The free limit of 3 followed by an upgrade matches the planned app.
- **Cross-platform products, 2023–2025.** "User login is required for in-app purchases as our company has developed products across multiple platforms, including Mac, Windows, and Android… Despite explaining this multiple times, it seems we are not able to get our point across." (Jun 2023, [forum 731471](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/731471)). "on iOS before buying subscription we are asking user to login to check whether they already bought subscription through any other platform… also to sync purchase with our server" (Jul 2023, [forum 733984](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/733984)). "it is a multi-platform SaaS currently running on web and Android… Apple is rejecting our app claiming that we should not require the user to register an account before purchasing a subscription" (Dec 2025, [forum 810103](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810103)). The same developer wrote in 724336: "I think we will have to surrender and create a "Guest login" button". — [PRIMARY]/[COMMENTARY]. Transfer: iOS ↔ Android access is the app's own justification, and it was rejected in all three cases, with no reported success.
- **2025–2026 cases.** Mar 2026: "For this issue, I have revised my app accordingly many times, but it still get rejected". App Review replied: "Thank you for your post and appeal. We're investigating and will contact you in App Store Connect" ([818946](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/818946)). May 2026: a game whose guest mode (device-ID account) could not buy got the template under 5.1.1(v) ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-au/answers/questions/5896293/apple-app-store-my-game-was-rejected-because-i-don)). Sep 2026: "The paywall literally refused to render for guests, so there was no path to buy without registering" ([PR #6](https://github.com/felipechaux/dado-match-shared/pull/6)). Oct 2026: "the app made people register before buying an In-App Purchase" ([PR #297](https://github.com/laduwan/passreadyprep/pull/297)). — [PRIMARY]/[COMMENTARY]. Transfer: the May 2026 case matters most. A silent guest account existed, but blocking the purchase for guests still triggered the rejection. Having the planned anonymous account is not enough if buying requires leaving it.
- **Phone calls with reviewers (China, 2018).** In Apr 2018 the reviewer said in-app purchases "也必须可以不登陆购买，将购买信息保存在本地就行" (must be purchasable without logging in; just keep the purchase on the device). When the developer raised phone changes, she answered "你怎么知道用户会换手机，万一不换呢？" (how do you know the user will change phones?). Then: "你可以诱导用户登录后再购买，但不可以强制用户去登陆" (you may encourage users to log in before buying, but not force them). The fix was a guest purchase keyed to the device, with a prompt on later login: "当前设备已开通VIP,是否与此账号绑定？" (this device has VIP; bind it to this account?). It was approved. ([Jianshu a68363b9fd3a](https://www.jianshu.com/p/a68363b9fd3a)). In Dec 2018: "登录操作应该是可选的，因为购买跟App账号没关系，购买使用的是 AppleID，但可以给出提示，告诉用户可以购买，但是无法同步到不同的设备上，建议登录之后再购买" (login must be optional because the purchase has nothing to do with the app account; it uses the Apple ID; you may tell users the purchase won't sync to other devices and suggest logging in first) ([Jianshu cbba20ce8821](https://www.jianshu.com/p/cbba20ce8821)). — [COMMENTARY] (first-hand, reviewer's spoken words reported in Chinese). Transfer: a soft "sign in first to keep Premium on all your devices" step before the payment sheet is acceptable if it has a way past it.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The sample is self-selected. People post when they are stuck, and few come back to report outcomes, which explains the 14 unknowns. Successes from reworking the app are probably under-reported compared with successes from arguing.
- [INFERENCE] Every case with a known positive outcome after a change used the same fix: buy without an account (guest, device ID or Apple ID), with optional registration afterwards.

## 2. Recency and wording drift: is the template still issued, and under which guideline?

### Takeaway
Yes. Seven of the 27 cases are from 2025–2026 (Mar 2025, Jul 2025, Dec 2025, Mar 2026, May 2026, Sep 2026, Oct 2026), and the last two are fixes merged in the past two months. The oldest is from 2015, under the old numbered Rule 17.2. From 2018 the template sits under 5.1.1 "Data Collection and Storage", with a Resources pointer to 5.1.1(v) since at least Dec 2022. By 2026 the header itself reads "5.1.1(v)". The "although guideline X requires… all devices" sentence cited 3.1.2 until at least Mar 2025 and "guideline 5.1.1" in Mar 2026, which looks like a template edit or slip, since the all-devices rule is in 3.1.2(a). The substance has not changed in eleven years.

### Cited Findings
- **2015 (Rule 17.2).** "User registration that requires the sharing of personal information must be optional or tied to account-specific functionality. Please make it clear to the user that registering will enable them to access the content from any of their iOS devices, and to provide them a way to register at any time, if they wish to later extend access to additional iOS devices." — [PRIMARY] — [forum 26828, Nov 2015](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/26828). Aug 2015, as quoted by a developer: "user registration must be optional or tired to account-specific functionality. Specifically, user should be able to access In-App Purchase before login". — [PRIMARY] — [forum 15505](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/15505). Transfer: the rule is older than Sign in with Apple and older than 5.1.1(v).
- **2016 (17.2, login wall).** "We noticed that your app requires users to register with personal information to access non account-based features… Additionally, the requested information must be relevant to the features." — [PRIMARY] — [forum 34622](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/34622).
- **2018–2021 (5.1.1).** "requires users to register with personal information to purchase non account-based in-app purchase products, which does not comply with the App Store Review Guidelines", "please make it clear to the user…", "although guideline 3.1.2 of the App Store Review Guidelines requires… such user registration must be made optional." — [PRIMARY] — [Jianshu, Nov 2018](https://www.jianshu.com/p/3b42411ff844), [forum 131751, Apr 2020](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/131751), [forum 688899, Aug 2021](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/688899).
- **2022–2023.** The template changes to "products that are not account based", "please revise your app to not require users to register before purchasing…", and "You may explain…", and adds "Resources: See guideline 5.1.1(v) - Account Sign-In" ([millionblog, Dec 2022](https://millionblog.org/appstore-review-5-1-1-data-collection-and-storage/), [724336, Feb 2023](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/724336)). The Feb 2023 version combines both triggers: "to access the app's content and purchase in-app purchase products that are not account based". — [PRIMARY].
- **2024–2026.** "iOS devices" becomes "supported devices" (Mar 2024, [747700](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/747700)). Mar 2025 has the header "Guideline 5.1.1 - Legal - Data Collection and Storage", still cites "App Review Guideline 3.1.2", and adds a resource: "Watch a video from App Review with tips for doing more for users with less data" ([RevenueCat 6041](https://community.revenuecat.com/sdks-51/handling-apple-app-review-rejection-with-revenuecat-s-keep-with-original-app-user-id-setting-6041)). Mar 2026 has "Issue Description… Next Steps" and "although guideline 5.1.1 requires an app to make subscription content available to all the supported devices owned by a single user" ([819232](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/819232)). May 2026 has the header "Guideline 5.1.1(v) - Legal - Data Collection and Storage" ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-au/answers/questions/5896293/apple-app-store-my-game-was-rejected-because-i-don)). — [PRIMARY]. Transfer: a 2026 submission would be judged on 5.1.1(v), whose published text is "If your app doesn't include significant account-based features, let people use it without a login" ([App Review Guidelines, Oct 16, 2026](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)).
- **Volume.** Apple's 2025 report gives 9,100,620 submissions reviewed, 2,093,244 rejected, and 495,673 rejections under "Legal", with no breakdown by sub-guideline. — [PRIMARY] — [2025 App Store Transparency Report](https://www.apple.com/legal/app-store/transparency/2025/). Apple said 400,000 submissions were rejected for privacy violations in 2024. — [SEARCH] — [Apple Newsroom (MG)](https://www.apple.com/mg/newsroom/2025/05/the-app-store-prevented-more-than-9-billion-usd-in-fraudulent-transactions/).

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] The move of the header from "5.1.1" to "5.1.1(v)" fits Apple tying this rejection to the Account Sign-In clause, which also requires in-app account deletion. A rejection now points straight at the clause whose test is "significant account-based features".
- [INFERENCE] Because the template has stayed stable for over a decade and is still in use, it is a standing reviewer instruction, not a passing campaign.

## 3. What App Review treats as "account-based"

### Takeaway
No Apple staff answer on the forums defines "account-based". Every App Review or DTS reply I found from 2023 to 2026 sends the developer to an appeal, a phone call or another thread. Apple's own definitions come from the rejection texts. The test is "features… associated specifically to the user". The examples given for account-specific features are "saving items for future reference or placing an order". Registering "for tracking users' purchases and game progresses" is acceptable as an option. Reviewers have said outright that a subscription, the user's records, server-side purchase status, and cross-device access do **not** make a purchase account-based. Their reason is that the purchase belongs to the Apple ID. The subscription case is named in the template itself: the all-devices rule is "not appropriate" grounds to force registration. For consumables and non-consumables, reviewers pointed to local storage, the Apple ID or a transfer code.

### Cited Findings
- Login-wall template (2019, 2022, 2023): "Registration must then only be required for account-specific features, such as saving items for future reference or placing an order." Nov 2024 version: "allow access to app content and features, such as viewing product listings, details, and pricing information, prior to registration or login. Once the user decides to use account-based features, the app may present the registration or login feature at that time." — [PRIMARY] — [114080](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/114080), [737212](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/737212), [683829](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/683829). Transfer: for physical goods "placing an order" is account-specific, but for IAP the template says the opposite, because the store account already identifies the buyer.
- Jul 2021, reminder app: "Regarding guideline 5.1.1, we still noticed that the making record and subscription feature are not account-based features." The fix was to drop e-mail login and use a device ID, and the app was released. — [PRIMARY] — [nocode-faq 1875](https://www.nocode-faq.com/t/topic/1875). Transfer: this is close to a tracker. Creating records and subscribing were both judged not account-based, even though they are stored on a server.
- May 2015, subscription app: "有料課金の前にログインを必須にしていたのですが、Appleからコラムや検索を行うのにログインをさせる必要はないでしょ！という指摘を受けました" (login was required before paying; Apple said there is no need to log in to read columns or search), and "課金状態をサーバーサイドで保持したいからログインさせたいという旨を伝えましたが、NG" (we said we wanted login to keep purchase status on the server: refused). — [COMMENTARY] (first-hand, paraphrased) — [Qiita](https://qiita.com/musclemikiya/items/be5423d86fa976d7cfc8). Transfer: "Premium status lives on our server keyed by account" is the same argument, and it was refused.
- Oct 2016, consumable content: Apple's message, as the developer summarises it, was "課金のためにユーザー登録を必須にしてはならない" (registration must not be required for purchasing). By phone, Apple then accepted optional login or a transfer code for multi-device access. — [COMMENTARY] — [teratail 53023](https://teratail.com/questions/53023).
- Personal information as the trigger. In 2015 the international version of case 3 passed after the developer argued he asked only a made-up username and password; Apple wrote "Consider rule17.2 rejection is resolved and we will continue with review" ([26828](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/26828)) [PRIMARY]. RevenueCat staff (Mar 2025): "Apple isn't rejecting you specifically for having user registration as we've had devs do this with no issues, but rather this might be due to the "sharing of personal information"" ([RevenueCat 6041](https://community.revenuecat.com/sdks-51/handling-apple-app-review-rejection-with-revenuecat-s-keep-with-original-app-user-id-setting-6041)) [VENDOR]. The Oct 2026 fix creates guest accounts "with a placeholder email, a random password, and no personal information" ([PR #297](https://github.com/laduwan/passreadyprep/pull/297)) [COMMENTARY]. Transfer: Sign in with Apple, Google and e-mail all carry personal information (case 16 shows Sign in with Apple counted), while the planned silent device account does not.
- Current guidelines: 3.1.2(a) lists "software as a service ("SAAS"); and cloud support" among appropriate subscriptions. 2.1 asks apps with "account-based features" to provide "either an active demo account or fully-featured demo mode". — [PRIMARY] — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). Transfer: Apple accepts that sync or cloud can be what a subscription sells, but no case shows this making a login before purchase acceptable.
- Developer's reading, Dec 2018: "非消耗型项目只能购买一次，而且在不同设备之间的同步是通过 AppleID 来进行的，跟我们 App 的账号没有联系，这样就更没办法要求用户登录了" (non-consumables sync across devices through the Apple ID, not our account, so there is even less ground to require login). — [COMMENTARY] — [Jianshu cbba20ce8821](https://www.jianshu.com/p/cbba20ce8821). Transfer: the lifetime product is a non-consumable, so it is the weakest case for an account requirement.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] A rule that fits every case: a purchase counts as "account-based" only when what it buys *is* the account service, such as a SaaS seat, a multi-user workspace, or content that exists only on the server. A purchase that unlocks features running on the device is not account-based, whatever the server stores. Premium here (more than 3 trackers, themes, stats, no ads) runs locally, which puts it on the non-account side. Server storage of the user's data argues for an account for *sync*, not for the *purchase*.
- [INFERENCE] No case treats a subscription more leniently than a non-consumable. The template addresses subscriptions explicitly and rejects them. For non-consumables, Apple's own restore makes the argument weaker still.
- [INFERENCE] I found no case where a reviewer wrote that StoreKit restore makes registration unnecessary. Spoken versions of that argument exist ("购买使用的是 AppleID", 2018; "将购买信息保存在本地就行", 2018), and the template's "it is not appropriate to force user registration to meet this requirement" says it in substance.

## 4. Accepted versus rejected: did explaining cross-device or cross-platform access work?

### Takeaway
Sometimes. Four apps were approved after a written reply, with no change to the account requirement reported: 2015 (an argument that no personal information was collected), May 2020 (cross-platform subscription, on the second reply), Dec 2022 (iOS ↔ Android status, citing Netflix) and Mar 2025 (an existing RevenueCat design plus redesign cost). At least seven apps that made the same or similar arguments were rejected again or left unresolved (cases 1, 2, 3b, 17, 18, 19, 23), plus case 24, which kept being rejected through 2026. The only visible difference: in the approved cases the free part of the app was usable without login, and the argument was a concrete cross-platform entitlement rather than security, anti-cheat or "we store it on our server". Reviewer variance is large. The same app in two languages got opposite answers in 2015. No approval came through a documented App Review Board decision.

### Cited Findings
- May 2020: an audio app selling a subscription valid on iOS, web and Android asked for login only at the premium screen. The first reply drew "Thank you for your response. However, your app requires users to register or log in to access features that are not account-based." The developer's report: "We have the app approved! We explained once again why we need an account for premium users as clearly as possible. We also added examples of other apps that offers the same content type and the same functionalities, all of them forcing the user to log in / register (they don't even have the skip option as we do - added after the reviewer asked us to add)." — [PRIMARY] — [forum 132680](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/132680). Transfer: this is the planned flow (free use open, account at the premium screen), and it passed, but only after a second round and a reviewer-requested skip.
- Dec 2022: the reply that passed read "We will be releasing this app to Android (Google Play Store) as well. Once a user subscribes on either iOS or Android, we want to reflect the user's subscription status on both platforms… we would like to manage information on whether or not the user has subscribed by tying it to the user's account. I think there are other iOS apps that have similar mechanisms, such as Netflix… But if it's not possible, I'd appreciate it if you could tell me why." Outcome: "3~4回差し戻しされて足掛け一週間ほど" (3–4 rejections over about a week), then "それで通過！" (passed). — [PRIMARY] — [millionblog](https://millionblog.org/appstore-review-5-1-1-data-collection-and-storage/). Transfer: the iOS ↔ Android argument is the one the planned app would make, and it has worked once in writing.
- Mar 2025: "We have explained to Apple that we have adopted a design that utilizes RevenueCat and that a major redesign would be required… and the Review has passed." The developer added: "there is a possibility that we may receive a similar point of view again". — [PRIMARY] — [RevenueCat 6041](https://community.revenuecat.com/sdks-51/handling-apple-app-review-rejection-with-revenuecat-s-keep-with-original-app-user-id-setting-6041). Transfer: same SDK, and a recent pass. The developer expects the issue to return.
- Nov 2015: the same app in two languages. The international version was accepted after a reply ("Consider rule17.2 rejection is resolved"). For the other, the reviewer "understands me but it is still needed to take action and i must change the system". The developer then appealed to the Board, and no outcome was reported. — [PRIMARY] — [forum 26828](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/26828). Transfer: approval on one submission does not bind the next reviewer.
- Arguments that failed or went unanswered: server-side purchase status (2015, Qiita); anti-cheat and coins (2020, [131751](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/131751)); anti-hacking and restore through the account (2023, [724336](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/724336)); cross-platform data sharing (2023, [731471](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/731471); [733984](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/733984)); the purchase being "linked to a user account" (2024, [747700](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/747700)); SaaS data on the server (2025, [810103](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810103)). — [PRIMARY]/[COMMENTARY].

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Arguing has a real chance, roughly 4 known successes against 7 or more known refusals in this sample. Every success cost at least one extra review round. The sample cannot give a rate.
- [INFERENCE] The planned app has the best version of the argument: a real Android app, one Premium across both stores, and a free part without login. It would still be asking a reviewer to waive a template that says the opposite, and the waiver has to be won again by every future reviewer.

## 5. What happens in practice: replies, delays, approved-then-rejected, bug fixes

### Takeaway
Small apps can and do get approved by replying in App Store Connect. The reported cost runs from one day (2015) to about a week with 3–4 rejections (2022) and up to about two weeks (2025). In 2024–2026, posting on the forums brings a stock App Review reply: an appeal link, "we're investigating", or a scheduled call. Approval on one version is no protection. Several developers were rejected on an update for a flow that had passed before, and one appeal on a login wall was refused (Jan 2025). Apple's bug-fix exception excludes "legal or safety issues", and 5.1.1 sits in the Legal section. So a rejection on this point may block a bug-fix update until the flow is changed (inference, not confirmed by a case).

### Cited Findings
- Timing: next day after a Resolution Center reply (2015, [26828](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/26828)); about a week and 3–4 rejections (2022, [millionblog](https://millionblog.org/appstore-review-5-1-1-data-collection-and-storage/)); post on Mar 11, "the Review has passed" by Mar 24 (2025, [RevenueCat 6041](https://community.revenuecat.com/sdks-51/handling-apple-app-review-rejection-with-revenuecat-s-keep-with-original-app-user-id-setting-6041)); more than 10 days and more than 10 messages on an update (2024, [761486](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/761486)); "審査期間は、1ヶ月程かかると予想しておいた方が良いでしょう" (expect about a month) (2015, [Qiita](https://qiita.com/musclemikiya/items/be5423d86fa976d7cfc8)). — [PRIMARY]/[COMMENTARY].
- Apple staff replies on the forums: "If you disagree with the outcome of our review, you may consider submitting an appeal to the App Review Board… Submit only one appeal per rejection" (Sep 2023, [737212](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/737212); same in Dec 2025, [810103](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810103)). "We're investigating this currently. A representative from App Review will contact you" (Jul 2025, [791387](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/791387)). "an App Review representative is scheduled to call" (Aug 2024, [761486](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/761486)). DTS locked the Mar 2026 thread: "Let's focus this discussion in your other thread" ([819232](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/819232)). — [PRIMARY]. Transfer: the forum is not a way to get a ruling. The channels are App Store Connect, a call, and the Board.
- Approved, then rejected: "Why with the same functionality on first submit they accept then on the second update they reject it ?" (2023, [737212](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/737212)); "First version was accepted and published… The problem is that my update is being rejected base on 5.1.1(v) even though that first version had the same design" (2024, [761486](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/761486)); "the previous version had been approved no problem. I made an appeal which was rejected for the same reason" (2025, [772174](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/772174)); "最近连续三次被这个审核规则拒绝，明明以前没事，审核通过了" (rejected three times in a row lately; it used to pass) (2018, [Jianshu 6504e6217abf](https://www.jianshu.com/p/6504e6217abf)). Apple, 2016: "we perform a full review of the app upon each submission" ([34622](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/34622)). — [PRIMARY]. Transfer: the app has passed review with a mandatory login so far. That says nothing about the next submission, and a release with a new purchase flow invites a fresh look.
- Bug fixes: "For apps that are already on the App Store or alternative distribution, bug fixes will not be delayed over guideline violations except for those related to legal or safety issues." — [PRIMARY] — [App Review Guidelines, Oct 16, 2026](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). The policy dates from Aug 31, 2020 ("except for those related to legal issues"). — [PRIMARY] — [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=84w3e5bm).
- Tactics reported: one Chinese developer in 2018 listed, as one option, a server switch that allows guests "审核期间" (during review) and forces login afterwards ([Jianshu a68363b9fd3a](https://www.jianshu.com/p/a68363b9fd3a)), and the 2024 V2EX author also mentions turning guest mode off after review ([V2EX 1037292](https://global.v2ex.com/t/1037292)). — [COMMENTARY]. Transfer: this hides behaviour from review. Guideline 2.3.1 targets hidden features, and the 2018 author himself calls the switch dubious. Not an option.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Because 5.1.1 is under "5. Legal", App Review could refuse the bug-fix exception for a 5.1.1 rejection. I found no case that confirms or rules this out. The safe assumption is that a 5.1.1 rejection blocks the release until the flow changes.

## 6. Likelihood for the planned "account to buy" flow, and what is safe

### Takeaway
The planned flow has three parts: free use without an account, a mandatory sign-in (Apple, Google or e-mail) to buy Premium, and a mandatory sign-in to restore from a no-account state. This is the precise trigger of a template still in use in 2026. A rejection under 5.1.1(v) on some submission is likely. It may not come on the first one, since some reviewers miss it or accept an argument. The approved precedents (2020, 2022, 2025) show the flow can pass with a cross-platform explanation. They also show it costs extra rounds and stays open to any later reviewer. The flow the template prescribes is the safe one. Let the purchase complete without sign-in, attached to the Apple ID / Play account and to the silent device account. Offer sign-in right after purchase or at any time, as optional, with honest copy about other devices and Android. Keep Restore working without sign-in.

### Cited Findings
- What App Review asks for, in its own words: "not require users to register before purchasing", "You may explain to the user that registering will enable them to access the purchased content from any of their… devices and provide them a way to register at any time", and "You may also choose to alert users that accessing game without binding an account may result in losing game progress or in-app purchase items." — [PRIMARY] — [731471](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/731471), [724336](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/724336). Transfer: "Sign in to keep Premium on all your devices and on Android", shown after the purchase or as a skippable step before it, follows Apple's own text.
- Fixes that passed or were merged: guest purchase on a device ID with optional binding at login (2018, approved); device ID instead of e-mail login (2021, released); guest mode with its own token (2024, approved); RevenueCat paywall always shown, "Restore Purchases (no account needed)" and optional sign-in (Sep 2026); guest accounts with no personal information, where "Buying or restoring now creates a guest account if nobody is signed in" and sign-up upgrades that guest (Oct 2026). — [COMMENTARY]/[PRIMARY] — [Jianshu a68363b9fd3a](https://www.jianshu.com/p/a68363b9fd3a), [nocode-faq](https://www.nocode-faq.com/t/topic/1875), [V2EX](https://global.v2ex.com/t/1037292), [PR #6](https://github.com/felipechaux/dado-match-shared/pull/6), [PR #297](https://github.com/laduwan/passreadyprep/pull/297). Transfer: the planned silent server account already plays the role of PR #297's guest account. Attaching Premium to it, then merging into the real account at sign-in, is the documented compliant shape.
- Blocking purchase for guests even when a guest mode exists was rejected in May 2026 ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-au/answers/questions/5896293/apple-app-store-my-game-was-rejected-because-i-don)). Moving sign-in to *after* the purchase did not end one review dispute in Jul 2025 ([791387](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/791387), outcome unknown). — [PRIMARY]/[COMMENTARY]. Transfer: after-purchase sign-in must also be skippable, not a mandatory step that holds back the unlocked Premium.

### Inferences
- [INFERENCE] Relative risk, from the cases: (a) account required to buy and to restore, as planned: high risk of a 5.1.1(v) rejection at some point, sometimes cleared by a cross-platform reply after one or more rounds. (b) Purchase without sign-in, with a skippable "sign in first" suggestion before the payment sheet: low risk. The 2018 reviewer explicitly allowed suggesting login before buying, and the template allows explaining the benefit. (c) Purchase without sign-in, with an optional prompt after purchase: lowest risk, and it is what the template prescribes.
- [INFERENCE] Restore gated behind sign-in is the same problem in another place. 3.1.1 requires "a restore mechanism for any restorable in-app purchases", and the 2026 fixes made restore work without an account. No case shows a rejection for gating restore alone, but none shows such gating passing either.
- [INFERENCE] The real cost of the compliant flow is technical, not review-related. Premium must be attachable to the anonymous server account (or RevenueCat anonymous ID) and merged into the real account at sign-in. The iOS ↔ Android carry-over then works only after sign-in, which the copy should say.
